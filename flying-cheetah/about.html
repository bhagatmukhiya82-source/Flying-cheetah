Our Motive & Manifesto

The Story Behind Flying Cheetah.

In 2026, launching a Shopify store takes 10 minutes. But running one profitably? That's where the nightmare begins. Founders juggle 6 different browser tabs: one for WhatsApp broadcasts, another for email newsletters, a third for helpdesk tickets, and their personal phone blowing up with delivery rider arguments.

We built Flying Cheetah to terminate this chaos. We combined multi-channel broadcasts, automated comment-to-cash DM triggers, instant WhatsApp VoIP voice calling, and 25-minute Borzo courier dispatch into one single autonomous operating canvas.
