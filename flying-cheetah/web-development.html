Frequently Asked Questions on Web Development in Mumbai

Have questions about building or rebuilding your store? We speak in plain English with zero confusing technical jargon.

How long does it take to develop a custom eCommerce website?

Typical project timelines range from 2 to 4 weeks depending on catalog complexity. A streamlined D2C Shopify store launches in ~14 days, whereas custom headless builds with ERP integrations typically require 3 to 4 weeks with end-to-end load testing.

Which platform do you recommend: Shopify, WooCommerce, or Custom Headless?

For most D2C brands, Shopify Plus is the industry benchmark for stability, security, and app ecosystem. If you need complete open-source database control, WooCommerce is ideal. For enterprise brands needing instant route transitions and sub-second load times, Next.js Headless delivers unbeatable performance. We evaluate your exact business goals before recommending a stack.

If we redesign our existing store, will we lose our current Google rankings?

No. We follow a strict Zero-Traffic-Loss SEO Protocol. Every existing URL is mapped to its exact destination with verified 301 redirects, canonical metadata is preserved, and structured schemas are upgraded. Clients typically see a 15% to 25% increase in organic traffic within 60 days due to dramatically faster load speeds.

Can you integrate WhatsApp checkouts and Mumbai hyperlocal couriers?

Yes! That is Flying Cheetah’s proprietary advantage. We pre-integrate our official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API (allowing shoppers to complete orders via WhatsApp) and pre-wire Borzo & Porter rider APIs so your warehouse or retail outlet can dispatch riders in under 25 minutes across Mumbai.

Do you provide ongoing support, hosting maintenance, and updates?

Yes, every project includes a 30-day post-launch warranty, followed by optional monthly engineering maintenance SLAs that cover security patches, Core Web Vitals audits, seasonal promotional banners, and continuous conversion rate optimization.
