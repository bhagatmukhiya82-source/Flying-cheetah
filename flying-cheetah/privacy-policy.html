Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Flying Cheetah Labs ("Flying Cheetah", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of merchants, enterprise clients, and end-consumers. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards for collecting, processing, storing, and safeguarding data across our autonomous AI commerce software, WhatsApp Business messaging, CRM webhooks, and hyperlocal delivery routing services.

By accessing or utilizing Flying Cheetah Labs, our API endpoints, or client dashboards, you acknowledge the terms established in this policy in accordance with the Information Technology Act (India, 2000), Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provide autonomous marketing automation and commerce services:

  • Client & Account Credentials: Full name, work email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, company name, and encrypted authentication tokens.
  • eCommerce & Store Telemetry: Store URLs, abandoned checkout events, SKU details, order IDs, and delivery postal codes provided via authorized Shopify, WooCommerce, or API webhooks.
  • Messaging Telemetry: WhatsApp, SMS, and RCS delivery statuses, interaction timestamps, and conversational state necessary for AI customer assistance.
  • Log & Diagnostic Data: IP address, browser metadata, operating system, and system performance metrics for security verification.

3. Meta Platform & WhatsApp Business API Compliance

Flying Cheetah operates in compliance with the Meta Platform Terms and WhatsApp Business Messaging Policies:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade end-customer phone numbers or contact data to third-party ad networks or brokers.
  • All customer communications transmitted via WhatsApp Cloud API are sent exclusively with user opt-in consent or in response to merchant order transactions.
  • Recipients may opt out of automated messaging anytime by replying "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE".

4. Data Security & Storage Standards

All data is stored in enterprise-tier PostgreSQL databases hosted on ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified cloud infrastructure (Supabase / AWS / GCP). We employ 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) guarantees that tenant store records are isolated and inaccessible across accounts.

5. Merchant Data Ownership

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of your store catalogs, subscriber rosters, and transactional telemetry. Flying Cheetah does not stake any claim over customer rosters or client contact directories. You may request complete export or permanent deletion of your data at any time.
