Do my agents need physical SIM cards or extra phones?

No. Flying Cheetah runs on the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud Calling API. Your team answers and makes calls directly through their laptop or mobile browser with standard headsets.

Can customers call us on WhatsApp for free?

Yes! WhatsApp voice calls run over internet data (VoIP). There are zero international or roaming charges for your customers, leading to a 60% higher pickup and completion rate compared to regular cellular dials.

Does it sync with our Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Yes! The moment a call rings, our sidebar automatically pulls the caller's Shopify order history, abandoned carts, lifetime value, and active courier tracking links.
