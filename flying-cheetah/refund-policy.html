Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. 14-Day Free Pilot Commitment

At Flying Cheetah Labs, we believe in demonstrating tangible revenue ROI before asking for commercial commitment. Every merchant account initiates with a 100% Free 14-Day Pilot with no credit card required. If you decide not to proceed during or after the pilot, your account incurs exactly ₹0.00.

2. Subscription Cancellation Policy

Clients on monthly SaaS retainers may cancel their recurring subscription at any point with zero cancellation penalties. Upon cancellation:

  • You maintain full operational access to your active AI agents and dashboards until the end of your prepaid billing period.
  • No further renewal charges will be debited against your payment method.
  • All historical client catalogs, order telemetry, and CSV exports remain available for export prior to account closure.

3. Refund Eligibility & Request Process

Because Flying Cheetah allocates dedicated cloud compute instances, WhatsApp BSP line activations, and automated runner microservices:

  • Platform Retainers: If you experience sustained platform service disruption attributable solely to Flying Cheetah lasting greater than 48 continuous hours, you are entitled to a pro-rata refund for the affected billing period.
  • Meta Conversation Units: Third-party telecom and Meta Cloud API fees consumed through delivered broadcasts and AI conversations are non-refundable once dispatched.
  • Custom Engineering: Web development and custom headless storefront retainers are billed against milestone deliverables approved by the merchant.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days directly back to the original source payment instrument (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit Card) via our payment gateway partner (Razorpay / Stripe).
